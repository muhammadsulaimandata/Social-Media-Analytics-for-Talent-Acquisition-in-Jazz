# Social Media Analytics in Talent Acquisition

### A Case Study of Jazz Pakistan

This was a university project we worked on for our **Human Resource Management** course at **COMSATS University Islamabad, Abbottabad Campus**.

The main idea of the project was to understand how social media and analytics are changing the way organizations attract and hire talent. We chose **Jazz Pakistan** as our case study and looked at how it uses platforms such as LinkedIn and Facebook, along with recruitment technologies, as part of its talent acquisition process.

## What We Did

We started by doing a **literature review** to understand existing research on social media recruitment, recruitment analytics, HR technology, and talent acquisition.

After that, we **collected and compiled information about Jazz Pakistan** and its recruitment practices. We looked at its social media presence, recruitment processes, and the role of technology such as Workable in managing candidates.

We then **analyzed the information we collected** and connected our findings with concepts such as the **Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)** and **Resource-Based View (RBV)**.

Finally, we organized our research and analysis into a **presentation**, summarized our key findings, and **presented the project to our instructor**.

## In Simple Words

**Literature Review → Research & Data Collection → Compilation → Analysis → Findings → Presentation**

Through this project, we got hands-on experience with doing academic research, working with a real-world business case, analyzing information, and presenting our findings.
